HOW IT WORKS

WHY WE MADE IT

Every time we wanted to share our screen in a call, we kept doing the same thing manually: minimize unwanted windows, close some tabs we don't want to be seen. Or if we are sharing a single window and wanted to show something else, manually switch and keep asking people to confirm.

Ugh, all this was too tiring. So we built the thing we wanted. Tell Nyx which apps and sites are private once, and forget about it. When you share your screen, those windows turn black for everyone watching while you still see them and use them normally.

THE SHORT VERSION

  • Works with Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack huddles, anything that shares your screen.
  • Lives in your menu bar as an owl. Lost it behind other icons? Open Nyx again and the dashboard comes back.
  • Add an app from the menu bar, or press ⌃ ⇧ L while you're in it.
  • Websites too: list youtube.com and only that tab gets covered.
  • Asleep until you share. Nothing is recorded, nothing is uploaded.
  • If something goes wrong, Nyx keeps the cover up rather than risk a peek.
