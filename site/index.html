HOW IT WORKS

WHY WE MADE IT

We kept doing the same nervous dance before every call: close Slack, hide the inbox, minimise the half-written message, then pray nothing pops up mid-demo.

So we built the thing we wanted. Tell Nyx which apps and sites are private once, and forget about it. When you share your screen, those windows turn black for everyone watching. You still see them and use them as normal.

THE SHORT VERSION

  • Works with Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack huddles, anything that shares your screen.
  • Add an app from the menu bar, or press ⌃ ⇧ L while you're in it.
  • Websites too: list youtube.com and only that tab gets covered.
  • Asleep until you share. Nothing is recorded, nothing is uploaded.
  • If something goes wrong, Nyx keeps the cover up rather than risk a peek.
